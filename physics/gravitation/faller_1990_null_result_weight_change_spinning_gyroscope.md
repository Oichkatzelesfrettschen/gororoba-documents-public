sults recently reported by Hayasaka and Takeuchi. No weight change could be observed under a variety
of spin directions for rotational frequencies between 0 and <math>2.2 \times 10^4</math> rpm. Our limit of <math>-0.025 \pm 0.07</math>
mg is more than 2 orders of magnitude smaller than the effect reported by Hayasaka and Takeuchi.
